# Teacher questionnaire (26 informatics teachers)

Online questionnaire reproduced in Lehka (2021), Appendix V (Додаток В), printed pp. 219-222,
https://doi.org/10.31812/123456789/7042. The thesis reports the results as percentages on printed pp. 83-84.
It does not state the administration date. Respondents were informatics teachers in general secondary
schools, some of whom also taught chemistry, labour training and technology, or mathematics (thesis p. 83).

**Released data:** the percentages printed in the thesis, in `revision/data/teacher_survey.csv`
(items T01-T09, `reported_sample` = 26). They are not converted into counts.
**Not available:** individual responses were not retained and cannot be shared.

English renderings were drafted with AI assistance and await verification by L. V. Lehka. The Ukrainian
text is the evidence. An asterisk marks a required question.

*English:* Fundamentals of quantum informatics for students of specialised (upper secondary) school.

Completing the questionnaire is anonymous.
