# Student assessment (45 students in grades 10-11, three schools)

## Data collection procedure

The procedure is reported in Lehka (2021), https://doi.org/10.31812/123456789/7042, printed pp. 18, 113 and 157-163.
- **Course.** The elective course "Основи квантової інформатики" ("Fundamentals of quantum informatics") ran for
  17 teaching hours (p. 113; roadmap in Appendix L, pp. 249-254). It was taught in the formative stage of the study, 2020-2021 (p. 157).
- **Setting.** Kryvyi Rih comprehensive schools No. 90 and No. 1 and Kryvyi Rih specialised school No. 9.
  45 students in grades 10 and 11 and three informatics teachers took part (p. 18).
- **Design.** Only experimental groups were formed (p. 160, Table 2.26). There was no control group.
- **Entry assessment.** An entry test-questionnaire (Appendix M, pp. 255-259; reproduced below) was given at
  the start of the course. The thesis classifies the result for each competence group as follows (pp. 161-162):
  - answers 0 or 1 to the required (closed) self-rating questions mean the initial level;
  - answers 2 or 3, together with substantive answers to the additional (open) questions, mean the intermediate level.
- **Exit assessment.** A final test was given at the end of the course. Its items are **not reproduced in the
  thesis** and are not available. Levels are assigned from the percentage score (Table 2.27, p. 162):
  - initial: [0%, 25%);
  - intermediate: [25%, 50%);
  - sufficient: [50%, 75%);
  - high: [75%, 100%].
- **Criteria.** The criteria and indicators for each selected competence component are in Appendix K
  (pp. 243-248). Each component is rated 0-3:
  - knowledge: 0 "has no ideas at all", 1 "has some ideas", 2 "has unstable knowledge", 3 "has stable knowledge";
  - skills: 0 "does not demonstrate", 1 "partly demonstrates", 2 "demonstrates with errors",
    3 "demonstrates confidently and without errors".

  The appendix lists the 41 selected components (codes СГК01.01-СГК08.xx), each with its expert score.

**Released data:** the published marginal counts of students at each level, for each domain, before and after the
course, from both the thesis tables and the chart caches (`student_distributions.csv`). Two documented
table/chart conflicts are kept unresolved (`discrepancies.csv`).

**Not available:** individual student records were not retained. The entry and exit instruments are not
equivalent, so no individual gains or paired comparisons can be computed.

## Entry test-questionnaire (Appendix M, verbatim with English)

English renderings were drafted with AI assistance and await verification by L. V. Lehka. The Ukrainian text is the evidence.

> Вітання слухачам факультативного курсу! Вхідне тестування-опитування необхідне для збирання даних щодо Вашої
> початкової обізнаності в окремих питаннях квантової інформатики. За цими даними, по завершенню навчання, набуті
> результати кожного слухача можна буде проаналізувати на предмет успішності. Серед питань є обов'язкові* та
> додаткові (уточнюючі). Відповіді на додаткові питання мають надати ті слухачі, які у попередніх питаннях обрали
> варіанти відповідей рівнів 2 та 3. Успіхів!

*English:* Welcome to the elective course! This entry test-questionnaire collects data on your initial awareness of
selected questions of quantum informatics. When the course ends, these data allow each participant's results to be
analysed. Some questions are required (*) and some are additional (clarifying). Participants who chose levels 2 or 3
in the preceding question should answer the additional questions. Good luck!

**Self-rating scale** used in questions 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 12, 13, 15 and 18:
- 0 - ніколи не чув (не цікавився, не вивчав): never heard of it (not interested, not studied);
- 1 - цікавився, але самостійно мало що зрозумів: was interested but understood little on my own;
- 2 - маю певні знання (уявлення) або уміння: have some knowledge (ideas) or skills;
- 3 - знаю основний клас задач і можу розв'язати деякі з них: know the main class of problems and can solve some of them.
